Things to confirm before building

  • iPhone web apps cannot ring like a real phone call. The 07:30 check-in arrives as a notification, and the call starts when you tap it. A ringing call needs a native app.
  • Voice needs a speech-to-speech service. Whether OpenRouter offers one is not verified. If not, the voice layer comes from a separate provider, and OpenRouter keeps the planning and text.
  • Microphone access in a home-screen web app on iOS is not verified. It is the first thing to test.
