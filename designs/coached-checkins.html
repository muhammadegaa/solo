Idea A

Coached check-ins for parents out of work

The AI handles the daily check-ins. A real coach reviews each person's week and replies by voice. The person, or the outplacement firm supporting them, pays for the coach, and the app is the tool the coach works with.

Who pays, and the first test

Who pays

Individual
About £25 a month for 4 weeks of coached check-ins. Price not tested.
Outplacement
A firm adds it to redundancy support, per person, per month. Not verified that firms buy this.
Why labs won't win
A named human coach and an outplacement contract. Labs sell software to everyone, not coaches' time to a niche.

4-week pilot, run by hand before more code

  • 3 parents, each paying £25 up front
  • Daily check-ins over WhatsApp. Wearable data if they have it, otherwise their own ratings
  • Coach review and voice note every Monday
  • Pass: 3 paid, at least 2 finish all 4 weeks, and at least 2 ask to continue

All names, figures and prices are examples.
