Four ways the daily check-in could look

Each direction shows the same five screens from the spec: Today, Nudge, Week, Details and Settings. The words and example data are the same in all four, so you can compare the format alone. Pick one, or name what to take from each.

  1. 1 · Fridge note

    Handwritten notes on paper. The warmest and least technical of the four. Numbers appear only on the Details screen.

    Cost: handwriting fonts can feel like a gimmick after a few weeks.

    Open

  2. 2 · Forecast

    Your day is presented as a weather forecast: overnight conditions, then the outlook in time slots. It is neutral and familiar, and it carries no judgement.

    Cost: the weather framing could get forced, for example a "Short and broken" night.

    Open

  3. 3 · Thread

    One ongoing conversation with tap replies. It fits your choice of a two-way check-in best, and it is the easiest to build.

    Cost: it looks like any chat app, and a chat can feel like one more inbox to answer.

    Open

  4. 4 · 24-hour clock

    A 24-hour clock with last night's sleep against your usual sleep window, and today's actions on the rim. It focuses on timing: bedtime and when you go outside.

    Cost: the clock takes a moment to read, and it is dark only.

    Open

All figures are example values, not your Polar data.
